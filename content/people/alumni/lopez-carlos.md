Carlos was a 1997 UM Arts and Sciences Minority Fellow. He defended his Ph.D. in 2004 at the University of Pennsylvania with Prof. Michael Klein, was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Texas at Austin with Prof. Peter Rossky, and then at Harvard Medical School. Carlos is now an Associate Professor of Cancer Biology and of Bioengineering in the Department of Cancer Biology and Center for Quantitative Sciences at the Vanderbilt University School of Medicine.
