He received his Ph.D. from UCLA with Prof. Michael Jung in 2001 and was a postdoctoral fellow with Prof. Peter Dervan at Caltech. Pablo is now Director of Sales and TikTok Ads at The Influencer Marketing Factory in Fort Lauderdale, FL.
