Jason received Bayer School Summer Research Fellowships in 2001 and 2002 and was an NSF-REU participant at UCLA in 2002. He co-authored two peer-reviewed papers with Dr. Evanseck, in the Journal of the American Chemical Society and the Journal of Molecular Graphics and Modelling. Jason received his Ph.D. at UCLA with Prof. Kendall N. Houk in 2010, did a short postdoc at the University of Pittsburgh, and worked as a consultant for the DoD. He is now Principal/Director for Business Development and Solutioning in the Booz Allen Hamilton Strategic Innovation Group, Washington, DC.
