Brooke Soike is a senior in the Duquesne University Honors College, where she studies Chemistry with a minor in Mathematics. She joined the Evanseck Research Group in March 2024. Alongside her research, Brooke is interested in the ethics of computational modeling and how bias in training data shapes who benefits from new methods. She explored this question as a Grefenstette Center Student Fellow, studying how AI drug design models trained mainly on one viral subtype may underserve critical populations where other subtypes are most common.

In the Evanseck Research Group, Brooke uses targeted and unbiased molecular dynamics simulations to study how the ionic environment influences the structure and stability of viral RNA across subtypes. Her work focuses on identifying non-canonical interactions that stabilize RNA structure.
