Jonah Babusci studied Chemistry, Biochemistry and Sociology at Penn State University, where he performed undergraduate research in earth abundant catalysis with Dr. Stephen Chmely, as well as sociology with Professor Mark Shriver. He earned his Bachelors of Philosophy in December 2023, after defending his capstone thesis under the guidance of Professors Mark Shriver, Richard Doyle, and Sarah Brothers. Jonah worked for the Community College of Allegheny County as assistant instructor and lab manager before starting his PhD at Duquesne. Jonah is excited to pursue his doctorate under the expert guidance of Dr. Jeffrey Evanseck.

In the Evanseck Research Group, Jonah is focused on Markov State Modelling of dimeric gRNA reorganizations, and the effects of base protonation and protein interactions on these reorganizations.
