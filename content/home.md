---
lede: We use quantum mechanics and molecular simulation to explain why molecules react, fold, and bind the way they do.
---

Our questions come from organic chemistry, biochemistry, and physical chemistry, and most of our projects are carried out hand in hand with experimental groups. On the chemistry side, we develop ideas about chemical bonding and apply them to Lewis acid–base behavior, catalysis, and the mechanisms of organic reactions. On the biological side, we simulate RNA and nucleic acid analogues to connect their structure and motion to function.

The group is part of the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry at Duquesne University in Pittsburgh, and is closely connected to the university's Center for Computational Sciences. Undergraduates are central to the group: many of our papers include undergraduate coauthors.
