That idea shapes how Professor Evanseck teaches. His courses use frequent, low-stakes feedback to build study habits, stories from the history of chemistry to make hard concepts stick, structured peer learning, and reflection writing to strengthen scientific reasoning.

He has taught general and physical organic chemistry along with graduate courses in computational and quantum chemistry and molecular modeling, many of them using Duquesne's research computing clusters so students practice on the same tools researchers use.
