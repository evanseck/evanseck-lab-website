### Graduate students

Apply to the PhD program in the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry and mention your interest in computational chemistry. You are welcome to email Professor Evanseck before applying.

### Undergraduates

Duquesne students can join during the academic year for research credit or through the summer REU program. No prior coding experience is needed; we teach the tools you will use.

### Collaborators

If you have experimental data that a model could help explain, we would like to hear from you.
