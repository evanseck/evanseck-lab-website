Duquesne's annual Graduate Research Symposium brings graduate students from every school together to share their research. The 2025 symposium was held in a hybrid format: an in-person poster session in the Power Center Ballroom, and virtual posters and video presentations on Symposium by ForagerOne during the week of March 17–21.
