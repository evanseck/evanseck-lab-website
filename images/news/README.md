Photos for the Announcements tab go here.

* For a post you write (content/news/), list its photos under "photos:" in the post.
* For announcements found automatically on Duquesne's site that have no photo, either:
  - add photos under "fill_in_photos:" in content/site.yml (they are used in turn), or
  - give one announcement its own photo under "photos:" in content/site.yml, with "match:" set to a
    few words from its title (for example "507,121" or "Presidential Award").
Any size is fine; the site makes smaller copies. Only post photos of people who are happy to appear online.
